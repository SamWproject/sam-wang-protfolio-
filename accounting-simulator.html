Assumptions

  • Tax rate default 20%; taxes are paid in cash in the period; no NOLs, no deferred taxes. If EBT is negative the model books a tax benefit (cash refund), a simplification.
  • Fictional company; illustrative dollars; no fees, FX or consolidation.
  • Asset sales are at book value (no gain/loss). Impairments and write-offs are tax-deductible and shown as a separate line within EBIT.
  • Debt-financed purchases are non-cash: the asset and the debt appear, but only the cash-paid part hits the CFS.
  • Amortization reduces Other Assets (intangibles). Deferred revenue is carried in Other Liabilities and is not recognized as revenue.
  • Not modeled: gains/losses, deferred taxes, interest accruals, leases, multiple periods of revenue recognition. Scenarios needing these are outside the engine.
